# Credits & licenses

Jimothy Simulator is an unofficial fan game (Jimothy belongs to nobody and to all of Seattle). Every third-party asset below is **CC0 / public domain**
(no attribution required — we credit anyway because these folks are great) or, for fonts only, **OFL-1.1 / Apache-2.0** (license texts are shipped next to the font files).
Assets we make ourselves (Blender models, synthesized audio, canvas textures, shaders) are original to this project and are not listed here.
Machine/human indexes: `public/assets/catalog.json`, `ASSETS.md`. Re-import Kenney kits with `node tools/import-kenney.mjs`.

## Audio — our own
Jimothy's voice (chitters, trills, purrs, hisses, squeaks, happy chirps, kit peeps), crowd reactions, cartoon screams, water/washing, glitch/slop
sounds, fanfares and most cartoon effects are synthesized procedurally at load time by our own code (`src/audio/synth/`) — no samples involved.
